Implementing the N2V2 (Noise2Void 2.0) architecture directly targets the checkerboard
artifacts and spatial frequency leakage caused by the standard N2V baseline.Based on
the original 2022 N2V2 specification by Höck et al., the transition requires three
fundamental changes:Deterministic Median Masking: Replacing random neighbor sampling
with the median of the $3 \times 3$ neighborhood (excluding the center pixel).

Anti-aliased Downsampling: Replacing MaxPool2d with MaxBlurPool2d to prevent shift-variance
and sub-pixel checkerboard artifacts.Architectural Rollback: Removing the topmost skip
connection (which leaks high-frequency noise directly to the output) and reverting
block-level residual connections to standard sequential convolutions.Below are the
drop-in replacements for your script to upgrade the pipeline to N2V2.1. V
ectorized Median MaskingReplace the _apply_vectorized_mask static method in
your StochasticPatchDataset with this deterministic median implementation. This
leverages localized index extraction rather than shifting full tensors, maintaining
 high dataloader throughput.
